Our Story

We opened Paceline in 2015 with one goal — to build the running shop we'd always wanted. A decade in, we're still hand-picking every shoe, layer and accessory for runners just like us.

01 · Who We Are

We're all
about the run.

If your aim is 5K or 26.2 miles, on road or trail, we're here to prepare, empower and equip you — from your first training run through to race day. Our feet-on-the-street range covers road, trail, track and field, apparel and accessories, with zero compromise on performance or style.

Paceline is small and independent, but the community around it is anything but. We run our own weekly club, host free gait analysis, and partner with local charities so 20% of profits from kids' kits go to Girls Run Glasgow.

Whatever mile you're on, we want to be your shop. Come say hello.

— Rae & Jamie, Founders

02 · What We Stand For

Three things we won't compromise on.

01

Curated, not sold to us.

Every brand we carry has been tested, worn and vouched for by our team. If it doesn't perform on a Glasgow winter long run, it doesn't hit our wall.

02

Community over transaction.

Free gait analysis, weekly run clubs, and race-day support. Come in for shoes, stay for the miles. Ask for Rae — she'll get you kitted up right.

03

Craft in every detail.

From how we tie up our store to how we pack an order — the small stuff matters. We're open a decade answering the details so your run is smoother.

03 · Ten Seasons In

How we got here.

2015

Doors open.

Rae opens a 40-sqm shop on Great Western Road with 12 shoe brands and a lot of hope.

2018

The clinic launches.

Free gait analysis and injury consults every Saturday — booked out in weeks.

2022

Weekly run club.

Every Wednesday, rain or shine. Started with 6 runners, now averages 80+.

2026

40 brands & counting.

Twenty seasons in, still hand-picking every product. Same team, same goal.
